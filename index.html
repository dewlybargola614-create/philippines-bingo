Philippines Bingo 菲律賓賓果

How to play 遊戲方法
  1. Ask for a number on your card: “Number ___, please!”
  2. Answer the question.
  3. Mark the number and the answer if they are on your card.
  4. 3 in a row? Say “Bingo!” and read your line.

Students open the link on their phones and get their own random card. 學生開啟連結即可取得自己的隨機賓果卡。
