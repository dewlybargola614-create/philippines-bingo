How to play 遊戲方法

  1. Pick a number on the screen: “Number ___, please!”
  2. Answer the question.
  3. Mark the answer if it is on your card.
  4. 3 in a row? Say “Bingo!” and read your line.

Students open this link on their devices and get their own random card. 學生開啟連結即可取得自己的賓果卡。
